# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors who come to read TekiZog's personal and technical writing, and to get a
quick sense of the person behind it.

## Product Purpose

Tekizog no Koya is a personal blog for recording life, sharing technical notes,
and preserving memorable moments. The home route is an immersive introduction;
the article route is the primary reading and browsing surface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing Astro content collection, article cards, sidebar,
  archive, search, theme controls, and responsive behavior.
- Keep the home page visually quiet so the existing animated cherry-blossom
  background remains the primary subject.
- Keep article discovery available from a dedicated navigation item and route.

## Brand Commitments

- Site name: Tekizog no Koya / Tekizogの小屋.
- Profile name: TekiZog.
- Existing cherry-blossom street background and avatar remain core assets.
- Voice: personal, warm, observant, and concise.

## Evidence on Hand

- Background video: `src/assets/videos/日漫的樱花街道.mp4`.
- Profile avatar: `src/assets/images/demo-avatar.png`.
- Real posts and metadata in `src/content/posts/`.

## Product Principles

- Let the authored background lead on the home page.
- Keep reading workflows familiar and content-first.
- Use transparency only where it protects legibility without hiding imagery.
- Preserve direct access to articles, archive, about, search, and GitHub.
